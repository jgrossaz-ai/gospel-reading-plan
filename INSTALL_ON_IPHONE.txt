IMPORTANT ABOUT INSTALLING ON AN IPHONE
This is a Progressive Web App (PWA). For “Add to Home Screen” to behave like a normal
stand-alone app, these files must be served from a web address (HTTPS). Opening index.html
directly from the iPhone Files app is useful for previewing, but does not provide the full
installed-PWA experience.

Once hosted:
1. Open the site in Safari.
2. Tap Share.
3. Tap “Add to Home Screen.”
4. Open the new Chronological Gospels icon.

The NLT text itself is not copied into this app; the app links to YouVersion/Bible.com.
